How a business gets onto OKX AI

On OKX AI you register by talking to your agent, not by filling in a web form. Vendo's job is steps one and two: turning what you already have into a compliant endpoint.

01 Connect what you have

Import an OpenAPI file, add routes by hand, or point Vendo at a form on your site.

02 Vendo makes it compliant

You get an HTTPS endpoint that answers with a valid x402 challenge, priced per call.

03 Register through your agent

Install Onchain OS, then ask your agent to register the endpoint as an A2MCP service.

Help me register an A2MCP ASP on OKX.AI using OKX Agent Identity from Onchain OS
04 List it and go live

Review finishes within 24 hours. After that every call bills and settles on its own.

What Vendo handles

The parts a normal business should not have to build

A compliant paid endpoint, without writing payment code

Vendo runs the x402 paywall using the OKX Payment SDK, forwards the paid call to your real API with your credentials attached, and settles to your wallet. Your API key is encrypted at rest and never returned by any Vendo endpoint. For self listed stores Vendo never holds your money.

Agent readable by default

Every store gets an OpenAPI file, MCP tool definitions and an entry in llms.txt.

Prices and tiers

Set a price per route, add up to three tiers, or sell prepaid call packs.

Signed receipts

Every paid response carries an Ed25519 receipt that anyone can verify later.

Uptime and levels

Vendo probes your upstream, tracks repeat buyers, and earns you a seller level.

The other side

Buying, from the page you are already on

Vendo Assist is a Chrome extension. Select text on any page, right click, and it finds a listed service that fits, fills in the parameters, and pays for the call with a capped wallet. It only reads the page when you ask it to.

How it works
Select and askHighlight a company name, right click, choose Ask Vendo Assist.
Vendo matches a serviceIt searches listed stores and pre fills the parameters it can read from the text.
You confirm the priceNothing is paid until you approve the exact amount. Per call and daily caps apply.
Result and receiptThe answer comes back with a signed receipt, and the cost lands in your books.
