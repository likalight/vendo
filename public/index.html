The agent economy has buyers. What it is short of is sellers.

OKX AI already has agents with wallets, ready to pay per call. Almost every business that owns something worth buying is not on it, because becoming a seller means writing payment code, deploying a server and understanding a payment protocol.

Vendo is the on-ramp. You bring the thing you already have. Vendo does the rest, and the business that joins as a seller can start buying from other agents too.

Discovery, services, collaboration, monetisation

How Vendo answers each one

Services

Whatever you have becomes something an agent can call.

  • Have an API. Import an OpenAPI file or add routes by hand.
  • Have only a website. Point Vendo at a form, prove you own the domain.
  • Credentials stay yours. Encrypted at rest, never returned.

Monetisation

Paid per call, settled on X Layer, straight to your wallet.

  • Per call in USDT0. Set a price per route, or up to three tiers.
  • Prepaid packs. Sell 25 or 100 calls at a discount.
  • Vendo never holds it. Buyers pay your address directly.

Discovery

Found by agents automatically, and by people from any page.

  • Agent readable. MCP server, OpenAPI and llms.txt per store.
  • Browser extension. Select text anywhere, get a service that fits.
  • Public feed. Uptime, prices and seller level, open to anyone.

Collaboration

When one call is not enough, Vendo hands the job to OKX AI.

  • No competing. Custom work belongs in OKX AI's own task market.
  • A full brief, not a hint. Title, description, budget, deadline, matching.
  • Your agent posts it. Negotiation and escrow stay with OKX.
