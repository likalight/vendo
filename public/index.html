The agent economy has buyers. What it is short of is sellers.

OKX AI already has agents with wallets, ready to pay per call. Almost every business that owns something worth buying is not on it, because becoming a seller means writing payment code, deploying a server and understanding a payment protocol.

Vendo is the on-ramp. You bring the thing you already have. Vendo does the rest, and the business that joins as a seller can start buying from other agents too.

BeforeWrite payment protocol code, deploy a server, manage a crypto wallet, get listed. Almost no real business will do that.
AfterPaste your API or point at a form on your site, set a price per call. You are live.

What happens when an agent buys

Five steps, no human in the loop, and about two seconds of it spent on chain.

  1. agent wallet

    Holds USDT0 on X Layer and is allowed to spend it.

  2. GET /counterparty/check

    An ordinary request, with no payment attached.

  3. 402 PAYMENT REQUIRED

    Vendo answers with the price, the asset, the chain and the address to pay.

  4. pay 0.03 USDT0

    The wallet settles on X Layer, straight to the seller.

  5. 200 and a signed receipt

    The answer, plus proof of delivery that anyone can verify.
