What happens when an agent pays

01 Agent calls the endpoint

It finds the service on OKX AI, or through Vendo's MCP server.

02 Vendo asks for payment

HTTP 402 with the x402 challenge, priced in USDT0 on X Layer.

03 The wallet settles onchain

Funds go straight to the seller's wallet. Vendo never holds them.

04 Response plus a receipt

Every paid reply carries an Ed25519 receipt anyone can verify.

What Vendo checks

Four sources, one verdict

Legal entity, sanctions and wallet in one call

Vendo resolves the company against the GLEIF registry, screens the resolved legal name against the US OFAC sanctions list, and confirms the wallet actually belongs to that entity. One request returns pass, review or block with the reasons attached.

GLEIF

Legal entity

Is the company real, and is its registration still active?

OFAC

Sanctions

Name screening against the US Treasury SDN list.

LEI

Verified wallets

A company signs a challenge to bind its wallet to its legal entity.

EDGAR

Filings

Profile and latest SEC filings for US public companies.
